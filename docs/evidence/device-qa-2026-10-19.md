   Needs a no-speech gate (Whisper `no_speech_prob` / avg log-prob, energy check on the clip) before any verdict.

3. **Learning curve**: lessons open with a question; owner asked for teach-first lessons and a Learn section. Design
   declinations (L1–L3, N1–N3) are in `design/explorations/learning/index.html`, awaiting approval before build.
