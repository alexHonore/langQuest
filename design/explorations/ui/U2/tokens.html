U2 · Night Market — tokens

Dark-first. Saturated jewel accents (magenta, amber, cyan) on a deep indigo canvas; glassy translucent cards; glow is reserved for speech states and the primary action. Light variant is derived by inverting canvas/text and darkening the accent text tokens while keeping the accent fill tokens close, so the two themes share one identity. Contrast ratios below are computed with the WCAG 2.x formula from the hex values shown (translucent glass is composited over the canvas before measuring).

Rules: accent text tokens (magentaText / amberText / cyanText) are the only accent colours allowed for text; accent fill tokens are for buttons, LEDs and glows and are measured against the 3:1 non-text threshold. Text on a fill always uses the paired ink token. Glass surfaces must never be placed over scene art without the scrim.

What animates: glow rings (opacity + scale 1→1.06), mic scale on press, LED colour, card entrance (translateY 12→0 + fade), lantern drift, mascot (Rive) and celebration confetti (Lottie). What never animates: text, transcript lines (they append without motion), the progress numbers. Reduced motion: glows become static rings, lanterns freeze, mascot idle stops, all durations drop to 0 except colour changes (120 ms kept for state clarity).
