All sizes scale with the platform text-size setting (Dynamic Type / fontScale) up to 200%. Serif display is fontFamily: 'serif' on Android (Noto Serif) and systemFont design .serif (New York) on iOS; no font files are bundled. Arabic, CJK and Devanagari fall through to the system sans; the serif is used for Latin and Cyrillic display only.
